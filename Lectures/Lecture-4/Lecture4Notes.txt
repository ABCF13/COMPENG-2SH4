Switch( value)
{
    Case condition:
        evaluation;
        break;
    Case2 condition:
        evaluation:
        break;
    
    default:
    evaluation;
}



The cases have a hierarchy that creates a domino effect, activating every case below it once its condition is met. The 'break' term circumvents it and disrupts the ripple effect, making all of the events independent.
