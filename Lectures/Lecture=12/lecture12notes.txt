& is the referencing operator:

& : %d -> %p


* is the dereferencing operator:


* : %p -> %d


officially called the indirection operator, it is Unary and applies to a pointer or expression of pointer type. The operator in english means, "follow the link to the value associated with this address"


if ptr points to var, *ptr is another name for var.

*ptr can be accessed and bypass conventional scope limitations.
